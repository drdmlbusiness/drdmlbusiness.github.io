Build lasting habits – a habit tracker with streak protection & no subscription.

Want to build better habits – but life keeps getting in the way? PocketHabit is the habit tracker that gives you room to be human.

BUILT FOR BEGINNERS

No complicated setup. No account. No subscription. Just open the app, add your first habit, and get started. A quick onboarding gets you up and running in under a minute.

HOW IT WORKS

  • Choose your first habits – or pick from ready-made suggestions during onboarding
  • Check them off with a single tap
  • Watch your streak grow – streak protection catches the days that don't go to plan

NOBODY IS PERFECT

PocketHabit's unique streak protection understands that real change takes time. If you miss a day, your streak doesn't instantly break – you have a buffer that helps you get back on track. You decide how strict you want to be:

  • Beginner: 2 missed days per week allowed
  • Easy: 1 missed day per week
  • Normal: 1 missed day every 14 days
  • Hard: 1 missed day per month

Grow at your own pace – and when you're ready, level up the difficulty.

ALL YOUR HABITS, ONE APP

Whether it's daily routines, weekly tasks, or monthly goals – PocketHabit handles it all:

  • Daily: Morning routine, exercise, drinking water
  • Weekly: Choose specific weekdays
  • Monthly: A fixed date or e.g. "every 2nd Monday of the month"

REMINDERS THAT WORK FOR YOU

Instead of setting a separate reminder for every habit, activate one global daily reminder. Open the app, check everything off at once. That's how simple a routine can be. Prefer individual times? Every habit can also get its own reminder.

YOUR APP, YOUR LOOK

Choose from multiple color themes and switch between Dark and Light Mode. An app you actually enjoy opening every day helps you stay consistent.

STATS THAT MOTIVATE

See at a glance how you've done this week and this month. The detail view shows a heatmap of your completions, completion rate, and your personal best streak.

  • Monthly calendar: every day colored by how much you got done
  • Weekday overview: see which days work best for you
  • Completion rates for the last 7, 30 and 90 days

MILESTONES THAT KEEP YOU GOING

30 achievements reward your progress: your first 7-day streak, perfect weeks and months, and even your comeback after a break.

A DAILY DOSE OF MOTIVATION

Each day starts with a fresh page from your tear-off calendar – one of 240 short quotes to help you keep going.

YOUR DATA, UNDER YOUR CONTROL

  • Export your habits as a backup file and restore them on a new phone
  • Pause habits in the archive – their statistics are kept

WHO IS THIS HABIT TRACKER FOR?

For anyone who wants a simple habit tracker to start small and stay consistent – whether it's drinking more water, reading every evening, working out three times a week, or keeping your home tidy. Especially if strict habit apps have ever made you give up after a single missed day.

No subscription. No monthly fees. Upgrade once and enjoy forever:

  • Completely ad-free
  • Access to all themes
  • Statistics history over 2 years (instead of 6 months)
  • Streak protection without watching ads
  • All future premium features included

BUILT WITH USERS, FOR USERS

PocketHabit grows with you. New features are only developed when real users genuinely want them. Your feedback matters – reach out and help make the app better.
